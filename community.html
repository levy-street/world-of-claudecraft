Your first quest

From newcomer to contributor in four steps.

  1. 01

    Join the Discord

    Introduce yourself and tell us what you enjoy making. The community can point you toward the right development, feedback, creative, or event conversations.

  2. 02

    Explore the repository

    Read the project overview, run the game locally if you want to code, and browse open issues or discussions for context.

  3. 03

    Choose something small

    A clear bug report, documentation fix, tested idea, welcoming message, or focused pull request is the best first contribution.

  4. 04

    Build in the open

    Share progress early, ask questions, invite feedback, and help the next newcomer follow the path you just learned.
