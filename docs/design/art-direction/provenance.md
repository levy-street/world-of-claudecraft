# Art direction reference images: provenance

Every image in this folder supports [`../art-direction.md`](../art-direction.md). All of them derive only from
first-party World of ClaudeCraft captures; no third-party game imagery was used as an input or is included.

**Source captures.** Taken on 2026-10-28 from `release/v0.45.3` in the offline client at the Ultra preset on desktop
(1600x900), staged with the dev tools: daytime via `/daynight day`, best-in-slot dev gear, HUD and nameplates hidden.

**Repaints.** Each repaint is one `gpt-image-2` image edit (OpenAI `/v1/images/edits`, quality high, 1536x1024)
of the source capture with the prompt below, then resized to 1024x576 (16:9, which undoes the model's stretch to 3:2)
and saved as JPEG. They show intended direction, not real-time renders.

## Prompts

### `target-warrior.jpg`

```text
This is a screenshot from our own original fantasy MMO, World of ClaudeCraft. Re-render this scene as a finished, polished in-game screenshot in the art style described below. Keep the camera angle, framing, composition, the layout of the landscape, the character pose and the wolf. Keep the character class and gear role (a plate-armoured warrior with a longsword and kite shield, or a mage in robes with a staff), but REDESIGN the character fully so it looks modelled, textured and lit by the same art team as the environment in this style: change proportions, armour and robe design, trim, materials, face and hair rendering, palette and edge treatment to match the style. The character must look native to this world, not like the original model with a filter. High-budget real-time game quality, not a concept painting. No UI, no text, no nameplates, no watermark, no logo. Art style: a two-treatment fantasy MMO look. THE WORLD (terrain, grass, trees, rocks, buildings, rooms, sky) is painterly naturalistic, like a gouache and watercolour landscape painting brought into 3D: soft two-tone shading with gently blended terminators, compressed mid-tone values, earthy palette with warm sunlight and cool blue shadows, strong atmospheric perspective fading to pale haze, wind-swept brushy grass, soft brushy clouds, subtle painted texture; keep each zone signature colour (for example violet flower fields stay violet). THE ACTORS (player characters, NPCs, creatures, bosses) are hand-painted stylized: chunky readable proportions with broad shoulders and slightly oversized weapons and armour, detail and lighting painted into their textures, crisp painted edge highlights on metal, a full value range from deep darks to bright highlights, slightly higher saturation than the world, a subtle warm rim light, glowing accents brightest on bosses. Actors share the same sun direction and contact shadows as the world, so they sit in it but always read as the strongest shapes in frame. No ink outlines, no glossy plastic specular.
```
