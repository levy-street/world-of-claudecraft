Visual and performance decision

Keep recovered Low as the performance reference for Windows review. Near scenery remains readable in the inspected views, but distant dressing is sparse, crowns are very coarse, and giant-tree sprites are pale through Thornpeak fog. What changes

Nearby scenery keeps its full geometry. Eligible small static dressing stops at each preset's full-detail edge. Larger scenery returns in progressively cheaper bands, with stronger Palmreach reductions. Compatible instance batches share colour draws; native shadow owners remain intact. Medium through Insane keep their existing materials, texture maps, lighting, AO and antialiasing. Actors, gameplay and collision remain outside this change.
