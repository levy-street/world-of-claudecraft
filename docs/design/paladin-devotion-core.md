# Paladin Devotion core

Status: playable foundation for Sunmender, Faithwarden, and Dawnreaver, including the shared
level 5-20 choice rows.

All numbers below are base values before attributes, mastery, critical hits, armor, or other
shared modifiers. Unless marked off GCD, instant abilities activate the normal global
cooldown.

## Shared resource

Devotion ranges from 0 to 20. Every specialization can generate it through configured
abilities that successfully damage an enemy or heal an ally. Protection also gains 1
Devotion from a successful block, limited by a 6 second internal cooldown. Devotion never
decreases with time. Death still resets the resource.

Divine Ascension consumes 20 Devotion, grants 5 Ascension charges, and lasts up to 45
seconds. Only explicitly marked abilities consume a charge, at most one charge per cast.
The state ends when all five charges are spent or the timer expires. No source can generate
Devotion while Ascension is active: direct abilities, blocks, ground effects, delayed impacts,
Avenging Wrath, and talent riders all remain at zero until the state ends.

### Devotion buff ownership

Devotion Ward and Requital Aura form one active aura choice: casting one replaces only the
other aura owned by that Paladin. Radiant, Dawn, and Grace Devotion form a separate choice:
each Paladin may maintain only one of those three. A Devotion from one Paladin coexists with
Devotions cast by other Paladins, and neither family replaces the other.

| Devotion | Duration | Effect |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Devotion Ward | Until replaced | 5% damage reduction. |
| Requital Aura | Until replaced | Deals 5 Holy damage when an affected ally is struck in melee and adds 5 Holy damage to affected allies' auto-attacks. |
| Radiant Devotion | 30 min | 20 spell power. |
| Dawn Devotion | 30 min | 40 attack power. It has a Paladin-specific identity and is not removed by Warrior Battle Shout. |
| Grace Devotion | 30 min | Restores 15 mana every 5 sec and reduces mana costs by 3%. Copies from different Paladins stack additively, with an 80% cost floor. |

Basic priority: use Final Edict on cooldown and add Dawnfall when it will hit a useful target
count. At 20 Devotion, activate Divine Ascension off GCD. Spend charges on Final Edict for
single target, move charges into Dawnfall for area damage, or retain one for Faithforged
Guard. The 45 second window means the player does not need to empty every charge immediately.

Verdict of the Sun God is a separate once-per-minute burst decision. The first two qualifying
hits only fill the sun and never bias its outcome. Final Edict as the third hit always produces
the single-target sentence, while Dawnfall as the third hit always produces the area sentence,
regardless of which abilities supplied the first two charges.

## Expected cadence

- Retribution reaches 20 Devotion in 50.65 seconds in the deterministic level 20 priority
  rotation when every generator connects.
- Holy reaches 20 in 38 seconds while alternating effective healing with Mercy Lance damage
  using its real mana pool.
- Protection reaches 20 in 52.9 seconds without block generation. Correctly timed blocks
  accelerate the cycle, while the internal cooldown prevents mass pulls from collapsing it.

The automated balance harness keeps all three ideal rotations inside the intended 35 to 65
second window without refilling mana between casts. These are first-pass targets, not
guarantees. Downtime, misses, overhealing, and movement lengthen the cycle.

## Feedback identity

- Divine Ascension begins with a dedicated salute, a rising gold-white burst, a sun nova,
  and a sampled activation sound.
- The transformed character uses brighter armor edges, a translucent light column, two
  counter-rotating halos, and one orbiting seal for each remaining charge.
- Healing impacts rise in ivory light, defensive impacts mix gold with ward-blue, offensive
  impacts use concentrated white-gold, and area impacts spread amber across a wider nova.
- Every action that will consume a charge carries a visible `-1` badge in desktop and mobile
  action bars. Its accessible label also states that one Ascension charge will be consumed.
- The final charge changes the transformation toward amber and increases the existing visual
  urgency without hiding actionable information when reduced motion is enabled.
- Verdict of the Sun God renders an empty three-part sun over the marked enemy. Each successful
  qualifying hit lights one segment. At two segments the sun pulses faster. The third hit lights
  the complete sun for a brief 0.2-second flash while the selected single-target or area verdict
  detonates, then removes it.
## Ascension decisions

- Retribution chooses single target, area damage, or one defensive charge.
- Holy chooses immediate throughput, group coverage, tank focus, or delayed emergency
  healing. Life Covenant improves for free so it cannot accidentally waste a charge.
- Protection chooses personal mitigation, group threat, ally protection, or holding a charge
  for the next tank buster. Sacred Challenge improves for free.
- Ascension is a strict spend window: Devotion remains at zero until it ends. Sacred Reserve
  and Perpetual Sun create the only immediate post-window momentum, and both require talent picks.

## Balance risks

- A 45 second window may produce very high empowered-ability uptime if cooldown alignment
  is too forgiving. Charge count, not duration, should be the first tuning lever.
- Retribution may overvalue Final Edict in every target pattern because its empowered
  explosion adds free cleave. Its explosion, rather than the base hit, should be tuned first.
- Holy can generate too quickly when repeated overhealing is freely available. Mana costs,
  cooldowns, and cast times are the primary cadence controls and need encounter testing.
- Protection's block generation is sensitive to enemy swing rate. The 6 second internal
  cooldown is the main safety control and should be visible in telemetry even if it is not a
  player-facing timer.
- Sacred Reserve plus Perpetual Sun can accelerate the next cycle; their row competition and
  the strict no-generation Ascension window are the primary safety controls.
- Guardian Covenant competes with core defensive charge spenders only for Retribution. Its
  30% empowered reduction may be too dominant in organized group play.
- Fixed healing and shielding values need to be checked across the full level curve, not only
  at level 20.

## PBE order

1. Verify state correctness: generation, 20-point gate, five charges, one charge per marked
   cast, 45 second expiry, death reset, time-stable Devotion, and complete generation blocking
   during Ascension.
2. Verify UI clarity on desktop and mobile: Devotion count, ready state, remaining charges,
   and empowered-button glow.
3. Measure time to 20 Devotion for each spec in single-target, group, movement, and low-heal
   scenarios.
4. Verify Protection block generation under one target and mass pulls, including shield and
   facing requirements.
5. Compare charge choices inside each spec. A charge should not have one universal answer.
6. Tune throughput and mitigation only after the resource loop and charge consumption are
   reliable.

No new Paladin choice rows should be authored until these six checks establish a stable
baseline. Before wider testing, update the branch from the release commit that contains the
automatic passive restoration work.
