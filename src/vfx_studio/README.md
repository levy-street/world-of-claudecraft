# VFX Studio art review

The studio runs the production Sim, Renderer, models, lighting and shared effect registry. Open /vfx-studio.html through the normal development server; production hosting also maps the root URL to this entry.

## Art pass

- All 287 generated catalogue entries now resolve through a curated identity overlay. Authored accents are used by the sequence engine. Existing bespoke Warlock and Shaman definitions retain priority, and studio drafts remain the final override.
- Frequent rotational builders use contact-scale effects; finishers retain the existing crescendo. Authored projectiles, motifs, exclusions, gameplay ranges and travel timing are preserved.
- Shaman heals use shaped liquid volumes with translucent depth, moving foam and narrow highlights. Chain-heal hops retain actual event endpoints. Nature heals open as leaves, holy heals descend through pointed arches, and temporal heals spiral inward.
- Transformations release open spirit coils; raising buffs use ascending arcs. Quiet stealth and barriers retain their authored presentation. Shadewolf has a dense silver-blue body and a short 0.65-second shell.
- Energy ribbons retain coloured shoulders and narrow hot filaments. Essential control glyphs have static bevels and full duration tracks. Simultaneous control states remain independent of decorative budgets.
- Ignivar frontal and soak surfaces have molten grain inside their original geometry. Contrast beds, exact borders, occupancy and countdown signals remain authoritative.

## Review tools

Nine classes, actual specializations and all six talent rows. The training party, live PvP duel and Ignivar practice encounter use real combat rules. Scene seeds and immutable talent configurations accompany command recordings. Freeze, step, playback speed, camera controls, movement, recovery and reset remain available. Camera changes redraw while frozen.

Build and scene settings includes local graphics overrides, from Low to Cinematic, plus reduced motion. Preferences are resolved before scene construction; the OS motion preference remains authoritative. Saved player settings are not changed. Reset/rebuild keeps a single context with serialized ownership.

Combat audio combines the renderer's spatial sound with the same canonical cast, impact, heal and aura cue helpers used by the game HUD. Cast loops stop on interruption, reset, mute, death and replay endpoint. The checkbox unlocks audio from a user gesture.

Draft import/export, captured frames and session export remain available. Replay repeats actual commands, seeds and talents; decorative randomness and asynchronous preparation do not promise pixel-identical images.

## Validation and limits

The review build has automated coverage for real casting, interruption, instant talents, invalid builds/seeds, replay endpoints, context replacement, simultaneous controls, actual heal endpoints, water pool bounds/expiry/disposal/prewarm, art-profile preservation and delivery integrity. Production typecheck, localization generation and build are run separately.

The masterwork pass adds eight sculpted spell performances, native gather/contact poses, temporary target materials, cosmetic elemental contacts, and 24 authored charge/release/impact sounds. Pyroblast and Chain Heal now use genuine offline Mantaflow simulations. Liquid auxiliary maps preserve rendered normals/depth/direct lighting and simulator-derived surface advection; they are single-view surface data. Low/Medium/High use 2K simulation beauty textures and Ultra/Insane use 4K. Source scenes, lossless masters and recipes are retained separately from the runtime.

The optional cinematic response follows actual presentation contact timing, clears on disable/reset, and respects reduced motion. Repeated graphics changes keep the original context, with bounded restoration retries and recovery before rebuilding after a failed reset. The Site's masterwork validation report records the two additional review cycles, all-profile motion checks, real signature casts, measured lighting grades, and the limits of repository-wide validation. This browser art pass is not a claim of parity with a finished AAA game.
