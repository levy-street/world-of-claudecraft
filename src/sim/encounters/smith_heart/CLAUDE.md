# src/sim/encounters/smith_heart: the Smith's Heart raid's sim

The raid's gating, its first boss, the Guardian of the Breath and the Guardian of the
Quench and the Guardian of the Heat (`docs/design/raid/smith_heart.md`), ticked once per claim by `tickSmithHeart`
(`seal.ts`), called from `instances/dungeons.ts` `updateInstances`: the Hammer Council first
(so its fall lights the Hammer vein the same tick), then the Breath's guardian, then the
Quench's, then the Heat's, then the Masterless Strength's, then the seal and the arrival, then the portal hops.

| Module | Role |
|---|---|
| `entry.ts` | The dev-only door while `SMITH_HEART_PLAYABLE` is false. |
| `seal.ts` | The heart seal's value, carried on the seal markers' template ids: the veins, the charge (READY, bit 128: all four lit AND the Ring clear) and the exit, derived every tick, and the portal bit (16) and the arrival bit (32), KEPT by the markers (the portal bit is set only by a burst, never by the veins and the Ring). `smithHeartProgress` (`ready`; `portalOpen` read off the claim's markers, so every consumer sees the sticky value); `smithHeartVeinWon` (the Hammer's vein waits on the whole council). Its tick also holds the markers non-lootable, runs the crystal's upkeep and BURSTS it: once charged, the first tick a living player stands in the middle circle (`seal_crystal.ts` `sealCrystalTriggered`) sets the portal bit and announces it once. |
| `seal_crystal.ts` | The heart seal crystal as a claim object, a leaf under `seal.ts` (it imports nothing of the seal, so no cycle): never `lootable` (there is no press: the owner's call of 2026-11-18), `respawnTimer` held at infinity (the generic object sweep never re-arms it), its round combat wall (the Hammer Council's pillars' mechanism: `COMBAT_WALL_SHAPES` row, the `kitObject` wall state, `syncCombatWallCollision`; the online mirror rebuilds it from the entity), the trigger (`SEAL_CRYSTAL_TRIGGER_R`, the dais's radius, and `sealCrystalTriggered`: a living player within it, the dead and released spirits never counting), `retireSealCrystal` (the entity leaves the claim and its wall goes the same tick), `holdSealMarkerInert` (the markers stay inert anchors). |

Rules:
- The Breath's push is the shared motion kernel's (an aura it reads, `src/sim/wind_drift.ts`),
  never a per-tick displacement: both hosts step it and the online client predicts it. A
  throw that can carry a body over a gap goes through `throwPlayer` (the knockback walker
  alone would seat it on the void's floor alive). Never name a wind aura `*_pull` (the Oath
  Chain's suffix, combat/paladin_control.ts).
- Deterministic: every pick is hashed (`pickMarkTargets`) or ordered (roles, roster, entity
  ids); the only rng draws are damage rolls, in claim-player order.
- Every visible state rides existing fields (cast bars and their targets, the holder's
  resource, auras on players and on the council, the pillar, rubble and rise-spot objects,
  `spellfx` cues), so the online client mirrors the fight with no wire or IWorld change; the
  pillars' collision rides the combat-wall view (`net/combat_wall_wire.ts` online).
- A new council mechanic lands in its member's module; a new difficulty is a
  `COUNCIL_DIFFICULTY` row, never a branch on a tier's name.
- Player text: no fight of the raid calls a raid warning banner (the owner's rule of 2026-11-03, made
  whole on 2026-11-09: its mechanics read in the world; the yells carry the `*.say.*` keys and the
  marked player's own short alert sits over the action bar, except the Hammer Council's: since
  the team's feedback of 2026-11-17 it paints nothing on the screen at all, every mark reading on
  the floor and the bodies); the
  damage and aura names are matched by `src/ui/sim_i18n.ts`.
- The heart seal crystal: one crystal at the dais centre,
  charged by the four veins and burst into the portal by the first living raider to
  step onto the dais (no press, nothing to click). A marker is told by
  `smithHeartSealMaskOf`'s parse, never by the `smith_heart_seal_` prefix alone (the
  crystal's template, `smith_heart_seal_crystal`, shares it). A new reader of the seal
  keeps the three layers apart: `seal_crystal.ts` never imports `seal.ts`.
