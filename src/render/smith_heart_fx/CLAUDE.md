# src/render/smith_heart_fx: the Smith's Heart's encounter effects

The encounter half of the raid's look (`docs/design/raid/smith_heart.md`, sections 5.9,
5.14, 7.1 and 7.2), hosted by `../rift_death_zone.ts` beside the dungeons' own fx. Everything is
read from IWorld (a guardian's cast bar, its events, the mirrored gate memory), so
offline and online look the same. The interior's static dressing and air are
`../smith_heart/`.

Rules:
- The Masterless Strength's quarter kits are actionable and drawn by `FinalKitFx` through the
  shared floor telegraph on EVERY tier (the threat colours; the Anvil and the Storm Orbs in the
  soak's gold), each dressed in its quarter's element (`final_telegraphs.ts`); the quarter it drinks reads on its body (`FinalFx`) and on the room's floor
  (`../smith_heart/heart_quarters.ts`) on every tier, the Overload bars in the HUD. The body,
  the arrival and the fall are presentation only (no sim state but the seal markers' arrival
  bit); the dust, sparks and trails thin on the low tier, the shakes go under reduced motion.
  The body's joints and timeline are kept apart from the kit's pieces so the final model
  replaces the pieces only.
- The Heat guardian's lane and ring are actionable and drawn by `HeatFx` through the shared
  floor telegraph on EVERY tier (threat colour `danger`, the vein's white-gold as the
  accent); its fire, sparks and smoke are cosmetic and thin. The Heat is the Hearth: its
  fire is white-gold, never volcanic red.
- Every floor piece of the Heat fight takes its rung from `HEAT_FLOOR` (never a literal step)
  and every warning draws through `HeatTelegraphs` (never straight off the kit): no two Heat
  floor layers share a rung across modules, and a pool sharing one blends commutatively
  (`moltenDecal`'s char and glow, the telegraphs' char and glow, additive curtains and
  sparks) or is one draw. Every floor shader projects camera-relative (`wocCamRelView` or
  `modelViewMatrix`): the instance sits a hundred thousand yards out. Pinned by
  `tests/smith_heart_heat_floor.test.ts`.
