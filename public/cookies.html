How regional choices work

We use a coarse country or region signal supplied by our hosting or content-delivery edge. We do not ask a third-party geolocation service to locate your browser, and we do not use this process to determine precise location.

Opt-in regions

In the EEA, UK, and other opt-in regions, optional categories remain off until you enable them.

Opt-out regions

In the United States and other opt-out regions, optional categories may start on. You can disable them at any time.

Notice regions

Elsewhere, a first-visit interruption may not be shown, but Privacy Choices remain available in the footer.

If the region signal is missing or unreliable, we use the opt-in experience. Your saved choice applies to this browser for 180 days unless you change it or clear browser data.

Global Privacy Control

Global Privacy Control, or GPC, is a browser signal that communicates an opt-out from sale or sharing of personal information. When a supported browser sends GPC, we disable Analytics and Marketing. Marketing is treated as the applicable opt-out from sale, sharing, or targeted advertising. The California Attorney General provides more information about GPC and state privacy rights.

Changing or withdrawing your choice

Select Manage Privacy Choices above or Privacy Choices in the Site footer. You can accept or reject optional categories individually. Withdrawing consent prevents future activation through our Site but does not make earlier processing unlawful.

When you disable Analytics or Marketing, we make a best-effort attempt to delete the related Google Analytics and Meta first-party cookies. Cookies placed or controlled by a third party may still require removal through your browser's site-data controls. Blocking all storage may prevent sign-in, saved settings, or other requested features from working.

Sale, sharing, and targeted advertising

We do not exchange personal information for money. Some U.S. laws define sale, sharing, or targeted advertising broadly enough to cover advertising technology disclosures. We therefore treat disabling Marketing, or receiving an applicable GPC signal, as an opt-out from those activities.
